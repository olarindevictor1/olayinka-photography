ABOUT THE BUSINESS

A professional photography brand focused on event, portrait, graduation, wedding and other lifestyle photography, providing high-quality images for individuals, organizations and events.

Olayinka Photography started in 2020 from a passion for photography and has grown through practical experience covering people, events and milestone moments. The brand aims to combine strong visual storytelling with professional service and high-quality image delivery.

Owner/Founder name:
Emmanuel Olayinka Salawu

Emmanuel Olayinka Salawu is a Nigerian photographer with a passion for visual storytelling, portraits and event photography. He began photography in 2020 and has continued to develop his creative, technical and professional skills through real-world projects and event coverage.

Currently primarily owner-led. Additional photographers, assistants or creatives may be engaged for larger assignments when required.

Mission:
To create authentic, memorable and professionally crafted images while giving clients a smooth, reliable and enjoyable photography experience.

Vision:
To build a respected photography and visual-media brand known for quality, creativity, professionalism and meaningful storytelling.

Qualifications/Certifications/Awards:
B.Sc. Microbiology, Federal University Oye-Ekiti (FUOYE). Photography experience since 2020. Additional photography training/certifications to be supplied if applicable.
